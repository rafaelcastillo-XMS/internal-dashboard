# Starter negative keyword themes (Home Services & Professional Services)

Starting points only. Always check each word against the client's services and the search terms data before proposing it: some words are irrelevant for one client and core for another (e.g., "commercial", "parts", "cheap", "training").

| Theme | Example words | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| Employment | jobs, job, hiring, career, careers, salary, pay, indeed, glassdoor, resume, apprentice, internship | — |
| Education / licensing | course, class, school, training, certification, license exam, how to become | Not if the client sells training |
| DIY / how-to | diy, how to, tutorial, youtube, video, step by step, yourself | "how much" and "cost" are research, not DIY |
| Parts & supplies | parts, supply, supplies, wholesale, home depot, lowes, amazon, kit | Not if the client sells parts or installs supplied parts |
| Free / low intent | free, cheap, used, second hand, craigslist | "free estimate" is high intent: do NOT negate "free" as broad if the client offers free estimates |
| Rentals / unrelated | rental, rent, for sale (unless relevant), template, sample, pdf | — |
| Information / reviews of others | wiki, definition, meaning, reddit, complaints, lawsuit | — |
| Out of scope sector | commercial / industrial / warehouse (residential-only clients), residential / homeowner (commercial-only clients) | Depends on intake |
| Out of area | city, county or state names outside the service area | Build per client from the intake |
| Competitors | competitor brand names | Only if the client does not want competitor traffic |
