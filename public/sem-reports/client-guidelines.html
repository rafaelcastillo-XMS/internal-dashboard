Client Guidelines & Best Practices

Turn more inquiries into booked appointments with consistent call handling, timely follow-up, and an up-to-date Local Services Ads account.

Updated September 21, 2026 · Shared guidance for all clients

01 · Answer and qualify calls

  • Assign someone to answer calls during your advertised hours. Arrange backup coverage and return missed calls promptly.
  • Introduce the business, listen to the customer, and confirm the service needed, location, urgency, and callback number.
  • Explain availability and the next step clearly. Confirm appointment details before ending the call; avoid promises your team cannot fulfill.

“Thank you for calling [Business Name]. My name is [Name]. What service do you need, and what ZIP code is the property in?”

02 · Follow up and share outcomes

  • Give every inquiry an owner. Record whether it was booked, pending, or not a fit, with a short reason.
  • Follow up on open estimates and unanswered inquiries. Share recurring lead-quality issues with your marketing team.
  • Review available call recordings to coach your team. Keep customer information within your approved business systems.

03 · Keep LSA requirements current

  • Complete the screening and verification steps required for your business category and location. These can include identity, background, license, and insurance checks.
  • Maintain your public, verified Google Business Profile and check that it matches the business linked to your Local Services Ads account.
  • Keep required documents current and address verification requests promptly. Check your account for requirements that apply to your specific services and region.

Google references: Business screening and verification requirements and How providers qualify.

04 · Review lead charges and account updates

  • Review leads, charges, and available recordings regularly. Google describes LSA billing in terms of valid leads; a lead does not have to become a booked job to be chargeable.
  • Use the lead feedback options in your account to report quality issues. Automatic credits depend on eligibility and are not guaranteed.
  • Before changing call-handling or account settings in response to a policy announcement, confirm the notice in your account or with your marketing team.

Google reference: How leads and charges work.

05 · Keep your profile useful

Review business hours, service areas, and offered services regularly. Share recent project photos and ask customers for honest feedback after completed work.
